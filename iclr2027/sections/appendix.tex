\section{H-DC protocol, pre-analysis amendments, and audit}
\label{app:hdc}

Design and analysis of the deployment test of \S\ref{sec:deployment} follow a timestamped pre-analysis plan (GATE\_PROTOCOL Part IV, amended by Part IV-A), under the project's governance rule: implementation gaps were first self-analyzed, then adjudicated with an external reviewer, then registered. We disclose the provenance of these timestamps rather than round it to ``pre-registered'': repository history shows the card builder was implemented (16:59 JST) and outcome-bearing rollouts had begun ($\approx$17:01 JST) before the plan text was committed (17:06 JST), and the plan text precedes the analysis-code and results commits (17:10 and 18:10 JST). That no outcomes were inspected before the amendments is author-reported, not independently verifiable from the artifacts; the plan is exactly what the timestamps establish --- an analysis plan frozen during construction and before analysis. Amendments, all registered before the primary analyses were run:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em]
  \item \textbf{3B control arm added.} The registration had assumed all controls already existed; the naive-truncation arm in fact existed only for 7B. The 3B \textsc{raw} arm (2{,}560 rollouts, identical cards/harness/seeds) was run as a registered correction, keeping E1 estimable at both scales and the Holm family ($m=6$) intact.
  \item \textbf{Paired-budget control made primary.} Realized \textsc{dslice} card length (mean 237.5 tokens) is genuinely shorter than the 300-token cap, so a ``shorter is conservative'' argument would not have sufficed: prompt length itself can affect success. The \textsc{raw\_matched} control---same transcript truncated to the \emph{exact} paired token count (per-card $|\Delta|=0$, 640/640)---became the primary contrast; \textsc{raw(300)} is reported as the secondary senior baseline.
  \item \textbf{Packaging disclosed.} Action copies are canonical dumps of the parsed action JSON (strips harness parse artifacts; 18/4{,}510 affected steps, with verbatim fallback wherever a tail could carry recoverable content); exact-duplicate non-mutating steps are deduplicated; the escalation ladder reaches stages $\{0,1,2,3,4,5\}=\{14,76,281,8,252,9\}$ cards. Claims therefore attach to the \textsc{dslice} \emph{package}, not to any single rule.
\end{enumerate}
Execution-environment checks, archived in \texttt{pilot/dslice/A6\_CHECKS.json}: a full metadata scan over every rollout row finds exactly one config hash and one vLLM/torch/GPU environment version per arm (the 3B pilot N/Q rows differ only in a GPU-memory-utilization resource field, unrelated to tasks, decoding, or prompts), with zero rows missing any audited field; the one post-hoc harness change (single-BOS tokenization, introduced for Llama) is verified token-id-identical on both Qwen2.5 sizes (pinned 3B/7B tokenizer snapshots) across first-turn and multi-turn prompt shapes with and without memory, i.e., a no-op for every arm it touches. Inference: aligned family-cluster bootstrap (2000 reps) with the primary Holm family $m=6$ \{E1$\times$2 models, E2$\times$2, HFR-noninferiority$\times$2\}; trap noninferiority uses one-sided bootstrap bounds against the $\pm5$pp margins. Full numbers: \texttt{pilot/dslice/HDC\_RESULTS.json}; protocol text: \texttt{GATE\_PROTOCOL.md} Part IV/IV-A.

\paragraph{Provenance-stratified sensitivity (post-hoc).} 188 of the 640 memories (29.4\%; A00/A01/A10/A11 $=52/49/43/44$) are deterministic reconstructions of the evaluator's oracle plan (harvest fallback), not model-generated episodes (\S\ref{ssec:dctest}). The E1 estimand uses the A10/A11 cells, i.e.\ 233 of the 452 model-generated cards and 87 of the 188 oracle-reconstructed cards. Re-running the aligned family-cluster bootstrap (2000 reps, seed 1234; outside the Holm family) within strata gives E1 $=+0.041$ [$-0.078,+0.149$] at 7B and $-0.226$ [$-0.347,-0.104$] at 3B on the model-generated stratum (36 families), versus $+0.424$ [$+0.255,+0.615$] at 7B and $+0.297$ [$+0.167,+0.473$] at 3B on the oracle-reconstructed stratum (20 families); the pooled post-hoc cross-model E1 difference is $+0.230$ [$+0.131,+0.338$]. The direct provenance interaction (one aligned family-cluster bootstrap over the union family universe---40 families, 16 of which occur in both strata---so the stratum E1s themselves are preserved and shared-family covariance is retained) is $+0.382$ [$+0.166,+0.606$] at 7B and $+0.523$ [$+0.306,+0.783$] at 3B (oracle minus authentic). Full numbers: \texttt{pilot/dslice/HDC\_SENSITIVITY\_FALLBACK.json}.

\section{External validity check (ALFWorld, completed)}
\label{app:alfworld}

A coarse effect-ordering check in ALFWorld \citep{alfworld2020} (Qwen2.5-7B; 3 task families --- clean/, heat/, pick-two --- $\times$ 4 siblings $\times$ 4 cells $\times$ 3 seeds, 144 rollouts, max 30 steps, env-verified win) maps pilot cells as N/R($\approx$A11)/S($\approx$A10)/X($\approx$A01 with the critical step merely omitted, not actively flipped). Verdict, reported without overclaim: \textbf{partially reproduced}. Aggregate ordering R(0.222)$>$S(0.194)$>$N(0.167) supports replay-superior-to-structural-superior-to-nothing, at n=36/cell with overlapping Wilson intervals---direction only, and the clean family alone reproduces the full pilot pattern (R=S=.33 $>$ X=.08 $>$ N=.00). \textbf{The near-miss harm did not reproduce} (X=0.250 $\geq$ N, paired flips 3 harmful vs 6 helpful): our ALFWorld near-miss is a recoverable omission rather than an active polarity/order contradiction, so a 7B model can recover the missing step from the visible goal text. Reading: replay-over-structure ordering survives a non-synthetic env, while harmful near-miss transfer appears contingent on whether the trap actively contradicts the task rather than omits a step. Full construction and deviation log: \texttt{pilot/external/EXTERNAL\_VALIDATION.md}.
